What makes it not-Stacked: cool white instead of cream, sans instead of serif, and the figure — not the headline — is the biggest thing on the page. What makes it not-TRM: no Inter, no cards, no rounded corners, no navy. What makes it not-AI: it commits to one discipline and holds it — every rule is 1px, every corner is square, there is exactly one type family and one mono. Restraint is much harder to fake than decoration.

—One decision left: the signal colour The chart bar and the on-the-line flag are the only coloured things on the page. That is the whole brand palette.
i · No colour at all
Pure black and white. The most disciplined and the most gallery. Colour appears only for a rise or a fall.
ii · Signal cobalt
One saturated blue, used sparingly. Reads precise and instrument-like. Risk: adjacent to TRM's navy.
iii · Signal green
Deep green. Unused by anyone in this market, and reads as money without being literal about it.

My pick: i, no colour. A property site with no brand colour is a genuinely unusual decision, it is the hardest to copy casually, and it makes the two places colour does appear — a price move, a school on the 1km line — carry real weight. Say the word and I will take this through tokens, every component, the charts, dark mode, the nav and the wordmark.
